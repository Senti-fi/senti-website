## Voice & Style Rules
*(apply to every line)*

- **Direct, never clever.** Plain words over wordplay.
- **Calm, never loud.** No hype, no exclamation marks, no token-launch energy.
- **Say what we do, not what we are.** "Spend from one balance," not "revolutionary fintech."
- **Speak to adults.** Explain without jargon and without condescension.
- **Reject:** crypto theatre, hype language, cold futurist aesthetics.
- **Feel:** Stripe/Linear credibility, with the warmth of a product built for a real person.

## Brand Quick-Reference
*(seeds the design system later)*

- **Colors:** Senti Blue `#007BFF` (primary) · Cyan `#00D9FF` (accent) · Navy `#0A1628` (depth) · clean whites/grays (clarity).
- **Type:** Editorial serif for headlines (banking heritage, seriousness); modern sans (Inter or similar) for body (clarity, warmth).
- **Name:** *Senti* = sentient (the intelligence layer) + the financial sense of value, feeling, meaning. Short, easy in any language.

# 5. Lucy Page
*(`/lucy`)*

## Hero

- **Headline:** Lucy. Finance that speaks your language.
- **Sub:** The AI inside Senti. Tell her what you want. She turns it into a clear, approval-based action — and settles it instantly.

## How Lucy Works
*(3 steps)*

1. **You say what you want.** In plain words. "Send $200 to my mum." "Move my savings somewhere safe." "Invest this in stablecoin yield."
2. **Lucy proposes the action.** In human terms, with the cost and outcome shown up front. Nothing moves until you say yes.
3. **It settles instantly.** No gas, no bridges, no waiting. Done the moment you approve.

## Intelligent, Never Autonomous

Lucy makes finance simple, not opaque. Every action is proposed in plain language and waits for your approval. Lucy never moves your money on her own. You stay in control of every cent.

# 8. FAQ Page
*(`/faq`)*

### What is Senti?
One account to spend, save, and invest in digital assets — with Lucy, an AI guide that turns plain-language intent into clear, approval-based action. Built so the benefits of crypto don't require learning crypto first.

### How is Senti different from a regular crypto wallet or exchange?
Most crypto products do one thing — a wallet, a swap, a yield app, a cash-out service. Senti coordinates all of them under a single account. You see one balance, one set of options, one approval flow. Lucy handles the routing underneath.

### Do I need to understand crypto to use Senti?
No. Senti is built so the underlying mechanics — networks, gas, bridges, protocols — stay out of view. You tell Lucy what you want in plain words; she proposes a clear action with the cost and outcome shown up front.

### Is my money safe? Who controls my assets?
Every action in Senti is approval-based. Lucy never moves your money on her own — she proposes, you approve, then it settles. You stay in control of every cent. *(Custody and security architecture documented separately in our security overview.)*

### What is Lucy, exactly? Is she making decisions for me?
Lucy is the AI inside Senti. She translates your intent into a concrete, executable action and shows you the cost and outcome before anything happens. She's intelligent, never autonomous — nothing moves until you say yes.

### What can I actually do with a Senti account?
Spend from your balance like a normal card, save in one clear place, and invest in stablecoin yield or digitized assets — all from the same account, in one approval flow.
